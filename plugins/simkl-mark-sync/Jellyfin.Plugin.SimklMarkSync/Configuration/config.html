Simkl Mark Sync

Sends what you mark played or unplayed, and every playback Jellyfin counts as finished, to Simkl. It uses the login saved in the official Simkl plugin's settings.

When you finish something you have already watched, record it on Simkl as a rewatch. Simkl keeps rewatches only for PRO and VIP accounts; on a free account the watch is sent as before.
